# Assistant questions

Questions you'd really ask the SpineFind chat assistant. Write them the way you'd type them,
vague ones included. One per line, starting with `- `.

Notes are optional. Add one after `—` if there's something the assistant must get right,
e.g. what you mean by a word, or what a good answer looks like. You don't need to work out
the answers; those come from the data later.

## Questions

- Which Spielberg films haven't I seen on disc? — "on disc" means a disc watch of my own copy
- Which films do I have that could fill in missing pieces for X actor? — films I own with that actor that I haven't watched
- what genres do I like the most? — "like" = watch most, from my watch log (there are no ratings)
- Can you recommend a film in my collection similar to X?
- Can you create me a double feature pairing with X
- Can you create me a double feature pairing in X genre?
- Is there any interesting Trivia around X film? — search the web; if nothing reliable turns up, say so rather than guess
- recommend some films rated 15 and under in X genre
- Which unwatched films do I have under 90 mins
- Which decade is best represented by my film collection?
- Given my film watching history, can you recommend me a film to watch tonight?
- What films came out on my birthday? — I'll give the date in the question (any date works, e.g. "on 14 March")
- What haven't I watched in ages?
- Which films have I watched the most?
- How many films have I watched each year?
- Which directors or actors do I keep coming back to?
- What is the average release year of films I watch?
- Given the films I have watched recently, recommend me something that us underrated and underwatched
- Can you put me together a 7 day/30 day watching challenge based on films I haven't watched?


## Words with a special meaning

House rules the assistant should know. Add any you think of.

- **Watched** (shelf): seen on disc, or marked "seen before, no date".
- **Watch** (log): any viewing, in any format.
- **Year**: the release year as I entered it, which is usually the UK one.
- **Like**: what I watch most, judged from the watch log. I don't rate films.
- **Trivia and facts beyond my data**: look them up on the web, and say when you're unsure or found nothing.
- **Dates**: I'll give any date that matters (birthdays, anniversaries) in the question; don't assume one.
